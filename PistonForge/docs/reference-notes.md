# Reference notes

These notes record the public App Store feature descriptions used to understand the product category. Piston Forge does not copy code, assets, copy, vehicle designs, branding or trade dress from these apps.

## RevLab — Engine Simulator

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/revlab-engine-simulator/id6817417979

- Physics-driven engine sound, haptics, gauges and 3D motion from crank/piston/valve/combustion/exhaust simulation.
- Wide engine family: V-twin, inline 3/4/5/6, flat-four, crossplane and flatplane V8, V10, V12, V16, W16, rotaries, race hybrids, electric research car, turbofan and radial.
- Manual, DCT, automatic and sequential gearboxes; clutch behaviour, stalling, grinding, drive modes.
- Drag strip with christmas tree, launch control, shift lights, personal-best ghost and time slip.
- Multiple listening positions, computed idle/cranking/limiter/lift-off pops/stalls.
- Configurable displays from analog clusters to digital race wheels, combustion cutaway and exhaust pressure views.
- Free tier limited by engine count; Pro is a one-time purchase. Offline support, English/Korean, portrait and landscape, accessibility notes absent at time of reading.

## Tuning Simulator

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/tuning-simulator/id6817126925

- Standalone ECU on a virtual dyno: fuel VE, lambda/AFR targets, ignition, boost target/wastegate duty, idle, warmup, cranking, acceleration enrichment and dead times.
- Closed loop, knock control, rev/boost limiters, auto-learn, “Fix from pull”, grid and 3D table editing, smoothing, interpolation, data-log overlay.
- Ten engines: fours, flat four, straight sixes, V6, V8; NA, turbo, twin turbo, supercharged, nitrous.
- Nearly 100 parts: injectors, pumps, lines, regulators, turbos, housings, wastegates, blowers, cams, pistons, rods, head gaskets, fuels including E85.
- Dyno holds any RPM/throttle or full WOT pull; power/torque/mixture/boost/timing logs; engine health with knock, over-rev, boost and heat wear; sudden failures.
- Career: rented unit, small dyno, stock project car, $1,500; 15 job types, four rival shops, eight-week season, repeat customers, warranty returns, intermittent faults, diagnostic tools, upgrades, weather, deadlines, rent and achievements.
- Daily challenge, Game Center leaderboards for peak horsepower and hp/litre, 17 lessons, launch control, anti-lag, flex fuel, drag night and rally stage.
- Free tier has Turbo Four and a guided tutorial; Pro one-time purchase, no ads/account.

## Engine Simulator (iOS)

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/engine-simulator/id6772016143

- Physics-based piston engine sandbox; sound and motion come from rigid-body simulation of crank, pistons, valves and combustion, not recordings.
- Build from scratch with layout, bore, stroke, cam and firing order, or describe an engine and have it generated on device.
- Live 3D, dyno power/torque, ECU fuel and ignition maps, rev limiter and engine damage.
- Global leaderboard for posted engines.
- Unofficial port; 23.3 MB; iPhone/iPad/Mac; subscription/IAP issues mentioned in reviews; user requests for turbo modelling, boost readouts, flat-six/flat-four and more gears.

## Mechanic: Car Tuning Simulator

Source: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/mechanic-car-tuning-simulator/id6744373316

- Build a custom car from scratch; install pistons, crank, gearbox, turbo.
- Disassemble/rebuild engines, suspension, camber, offsets.
- Paint, vinyls, body kits, rims, spoilers.
- Drift, sprint, drag, time attack and free roam in an open world.
- Nitro, gearbox, tires, turbo upgrades affect performance; career story, bosses, photo mode, 15+ languages and ads-heavy monetisation.

## Product implications for Piston Forge

- The category rewards computed sound/motion, a broad engine catalogue, deep ECU tables, a visible dyno, and understandable failure consequences.
- Piston Forge's differentiator is combining a tactile engine-assembly bench, a fully editable ECU, a live quarter-mile and handling test, and a contract economy in one coherent 3D workshop, with all assets procedurally authored.
- No online leaderboard, account or payments were selected in the approved Blueprint.
