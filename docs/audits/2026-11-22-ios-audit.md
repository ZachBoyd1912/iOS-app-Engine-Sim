# iOS audits — 22/11/2026

Three read-only agents audited the native SwiftUI app (`Native/App/**`) on the evening of
22/11/2026, the same night the audio crash was fixed and 0.2.1 went onto Zach's iPhone 13.
These are *findings only* — nothing here has been implemented yet.

Scope note: `PistonForge/ios` is the old Capacitor web shell and was excluded. The
"Accessibility audit 40%" line in `docs/implementation-status.md` refers to that older build,
not this code.

---

## P0 — breaks platform expectations

1. **Landscape clips the app's identity and its only settings entry.** `EngineScreen.swift:21-32`
   keeps the portrait stack in landscape; the engine name is off-screen, the spec line is cut and
   the gear button is half off the right edge. Landscape is declared in `Native/project.yml:43`.
   Fix: branch on `verticalSizeClass == .compact` into an `HStack` cockpit, stop ignoring the
   horizontal safe area for content (clip only the 3D canvas).
2. **The primary action is unreachable on a 4.7" iPhone.** Same fixed `VStack`, no `ScrollView`,
   no `ViewThatFits`. Content needs ~645 pt; a 4.7" screen gives ~598 pt after chrome, so
   Start/Stop falls off. Fix: `ScrollView` + `.safeAreaInset(edge: .bottom)` for the controls.
3. **"Reset the garage" wipes all builds with no confirmation and promises an undo that does not
   exist.** `RootView.swift:158` assigns `SaveFile.defaults()` at once; `SettingsScreen.swift:54-56`
   says "You can undo it for 10 seconds". Swipe-delete in `RootView.swift:187-189` has no undo
   either, and `GameStore.delete` silently refuses when one build remains.
4. **Four of five Settings controls do nothing.** Steering, Gears, Haptics and 3D quality are
   written to `SaveFile` and never read (only `settings.sound` is wired). The footer even admits
   "Used when driving arrives in the next update" while Drive ships.

## P1 — visibly unpolished

- **No back path anywhere.** There is not one `NavigationLink`, `.sheet` or `.alert` in the app;
  Settings is a Garage *segment*, entered from the Engine gear, and the tab bar silently changes.
  Recommended: Garage becomes a `List` hub with `NavigationLink` chevrons; Settings becomes a
  sheet with Done.
- **A 4-way segmented control is used as top-level navigation** (`RootView.swift:131-136`), and a
  segmented control is ~32 pt tall, under 44.
- **Title styles disagree** between tabs (large on Tune/Dyno, inline on Drive/Garage, hidden on
  Engine) and two titles compete on Garage.
- **Tapping a part in 3D discards the selection** (`RootView.swift:73-76` uses `{ _ in }`) and
  lands on the top of the Parts list.
- **Dyno has no progress, disabled or empty state**; the button stays enabled and four empty
  charts render before the first pull.
- **Save recovery is invisible** — `.recoveredFromBackup` / `.corruptReset` are captured and never
  shown, and write errors are swallowed with `try?`.
- **Zero motion in the entire app** (no `withAnimation`/`.transition`/`.contentTransition`).
- **Dynamic Type breaks the dense surfaces** — 38×26 pt table cells clip `caption2` at large sizes.
- **VoiceOver cannot operate the core interactions** (see accessibility section).
- **No disabled appearance** on the shared `PrimaryButton`.

## Accessibility — the five that matter most

1. **`Readout` is not a usable accessibility element** (`Components.swift:105-134`). The value sits
   inside the *label*, the status icon leaks its SF Symbol name, there is no `.accessibilityValue`,
   and the label is rebuilt 60×/second by `GameStore.run()`, so speech is interrupted constantly.
   One struct fixes RPM/HP/AFR/boost on three screens.
2. **The 3D engine has no non-visual equivalent** (`EngineViewport.swift:84-86`) — it announces a
   hint describing drag/pinch/tap, none of which VoiceOver can perform, and part selection is
   unreachable.
3. **The pedals are unusable with VoiceOver** (`DriveTab.swift:198-213`) — a `DragGesture` with
   `.isButton` and no action: activation blips one frame, the car never moves.
4. **The tune table throws away every cell value** (`TuneScreen.swift:108-110`) with
   `children: .ignore`, on the densest screen in the app.
5. **Two settings the app already promises are unimplemented** — `settings.haptics` has zero
   consumers, and `EngineAnimationSystem` runs a continuous full-screen animation with no
   `accessibilityReduceMotion` gate (WCAG 2.2.2).

Also: hot tune cells reach only 4.22:1 contrast (AA needs 4.5), power and torque are distinguished
by hue alone on the dyno chart, and the knock/lean warnings are never announced.

## Highest perceived-quality wins, cheapest first

1. Real navigation hierarchy (Garage list hub + Settings sheet) — deletes the fake segmented nav
   and gives every screen a back path.
2. Adaptive Engine screen (landscape cockpit + scrollable body with pinned controls).
3. Stop lying, start confirming: real 10-second undo or a confirmation dialog, and wire or disable
   the dead Settings controls.
4. Motion + state pass on Dyno and Drive (disabled button, inline progress, empty state, snappy
   transitions, `contentTransition(.numericText())`).
5. One type scale and one surface scale (`@ScaledMetric`, semantic text styles, one card/row fill,
   16 pt margins everywhere).
