Read-only audit by a Haiku agent on 15/11/2026 against the ZB UX standard and the economy numbers in `src/sim/`. Findings are the agent's; each one must be checked against the code before it is acted on.

## Career economy

### [high] Fitting a part keeps the old fuel/ignition map, which was tuned for the stock engine. The first dyno pull with that map leans out and seizes the engine. A failed pull sets condition to 0 whatever the severity. On the starter (condition 58) a single pull after a $850 sport cam seizes it. Recovery costs a rebuild of 420 + 0.34 x 850 = $709.
- Where: src/app.ts installPart (about line 536-548: build.ecu = sanitizeEcu(build.ecu, build.engine)); src/app.ts commit (about line 496-523: ECU reset to defaultEcu only when layout/bore/stroke change); src/sim/performance.ts:204-205 (failed pull sets conditionAfter = 0)
- Numbers: Starter + sport cam ($850), map kept: dynoPull valid=false, failure=lean, afr 11.4-17.3, knock peak 48.7, conditionAfter 0 (wear 58). Same part with defaultEcu: 105 hp, valid. Bundle A (p98, S1 cam, P1 head, P1 intake, S1 exhaust, 440 cc, 255 pump; $3,290): map kept seizes (lean, afr to 19.8); reset map 118 hp valid. Turbo-S alone ($1,100): map kept 86 hp (no boost gain); reset map 118 hp. Bundle B ($5,390, exceeds the $4,800 starting cash by $590): map kept seizes; reset map 165 hp. Starter stock rebuild $420; after the cam, rebuild $709.
- Suggestion: Reset or re-map the ECU when a part that changes airflow or fuel is fitted, or show a warning and require a confirm before the first pull. Scale failure damage with severity instead of setting condition to 0.

### [high] Nitrous is the cheapest horsepower in the game by a wide margin. It adds 90 hp on the untouched starter map with a valid pull and no fuel or injector work. Its cost is one-off in engineCost. I found no per-use cost in the sections read, so the gap to turbo and cams is very large.
- Where: src/sim/data.ts:160 (nitrous: cost 1300, unlockRep 40, nitrousHp 90); src/sim/engine.ts:141 (engineCost adds the one-off cost); src/sim/performance.ts (torqueCurve, not read in full)
- Numbers: Nitrous alone ($1,300): game path 176 hp (+90), valid, wear 0.4, knock peak 7.1, afr 11.4-13.6, unlock rep 40 (reached after two contracts). Hp per $1k: nitrous 69.2 (default ECU), turbo-S 29.1, Turbo M 16.8, Sport cam 22.4, Pro race cam 12.8, CNC head 5.7. Nitrous + p98 + 440 cc + 255 pump ($2,020): 176 hp, valid.
- Suggestion: Check whether nitrous has a per-shot or bottle cost in torqueCurve/dynoPull. If not, add a per-use cost or require fuel and injector headroom, and re-price it toward the turbo-S value.

The supercar contract (C-101) asks the player to tune and deliver a car they cannot own. Affordability by stage shows the body only enters the reachable set through the 'other' bodies.
