## The short version

Piston Forge does not collect personal data. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no
advertising and no crash-reporting service. The only information the game keeps is your save
file, and it stays on your device.

## What the game keeps on your device

Your save file holds:

- career progress: cash, reputation, the car bodies you own, your saved engine builds, and your best dyno, drag strip and handling course results
- your Sandbox build
- your settings: reduced motion, automatic shifting, and vibration on or off

The save is kept in the app's own storage on your device. On an iPhone, the app also keeps a
copy in its native settings storage, so the save can be restored if the in-app storage is
cleared. The game keeps a backup copy of the last save that loaded correctly.

## The save never leaves your device

We run no server that receives your save. Piston Forge does not connect to the internet to
play. It does not send your save, your settings or any usage information to us or to anyone
else.

If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or to a computer, Apple's backup may include the app's
stored data. That is controlled by your backup settings with Apple. We do not receive it.

## What we do not collect

We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers,
purchase history or usage data. The app does not ask for access to your camera, microphone,
location or contacts.

## Your choices

- **Reset all progress:** open Setup in the game and tap Reset all progress. This deletes the save from your device.
- **Delete the app:** removing Piston Forge from your iPhone removes the app from that device, along with the data it stored there.

## Children

We do not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to give you, correct or delete.
For the save on your device, use the choices above.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date at the top of this page. If the game
ever starts to collect data, we will update this policy before that change takes effect.
